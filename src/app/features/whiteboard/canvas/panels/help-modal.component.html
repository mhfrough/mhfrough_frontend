Getting started

The whiteboard is an infinite canvas for sketching ideas, diagrams, notes and plans. Pick a tool from the bottom toolbar (or press its shortcut key), then click-and-drag on the canvas to draw. Press V (or finish a shape) to return to the Selection tool. Everything you do is saved automatically — see Saving & versions below.

Drawing & shapes

Rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, triangles, polygons and stars are drag-to-draw: press down where one corner should be and release at the opposite corner. Hold Shift while dragging to keep proportions square; the Circle and the Rounded Rectangle presets do the same thing without the modifier. Lines and arrows snap to 15° angles when Shift is held. Pencil, Brush and Highlighter draw freehand strokes. The pin button at the right end of the toolbar keeps the current tool active after each drawing, for placing many shapes in a row.

Selecting, moving & styling

With the Selection tool, click an element to select it, drag to move it, and use the handles to resize or rotate. Shift-click adds to the selection; dragging on empty canvas draws a selection box. The left-hand panel shows position, size, stroke, fill, stroke width, line style and opacity for whatever is selected — or the defaults for the next thing you draw. Right-click any element for duplicate, z-order, align, lock and delete actions. Group a multi-selection with Ctrl+G.

Text, sticky notes & code blocks

The Text tool places a text box with a single click; Sticky drops a classic note card; Code Block creates a monospace card for snippets. Double-click any of them later to edit. While editing, the style panel offers font, size, weight, bold/italic/underline, per-selection color, alignment and checklists — and typed or pasted URLs turn into real links (Ctrl+click to open). A text box left empty is discarded automatically when you click away.

AI writing assistant

While editing text or a sticky note, a small pill floats above the box with three actions: Improve rewrites for clarity, Shorten condenses, and Fix corrects spelling and grammar only. The rewrite replaces your text in place and a single Ctrl+Z brings the original back. (Requires AI assistance to be enabled on this site; if it isn't, the buttons say so.)

Images

Use the Image tool, paste from the clipboard, or drag files straight onto the canvas (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, SVG — up to 5 MB each). Large photos are automatically downscaled and compressed for storage. Select an image to flip or crop it from the context toolbar.

Frames, layers & navigation

Frames (F) are labelled regions for organising a big board. The Layers panel (bottom-right) lists every element: drag to reorder stacking, rename with a double-click, and lock or hide individual items. Zoom with the mouse wheel, pan with Space+drag or the middle mouse button, and use the minimap to jump around a large board. Grid, snapping and rulers toggle from the bottom-right buttons; Ctrl+0 resets the view.

Saving & versions

The board autosaves to this browser moments after every change — the indicator next to the top-left menus shows when. Versions lets you snapshot the current state with a label and restore or delete snapshots later (the 30 most recent are kept). When the site owner is signed in, the board also syncs to their account in the cloud; the indicator's icon shows which tier you're on. If local storage ever fills up, a red "Not saved" warning appears instead of silently losing work.

Export, import & print

Export offers PNG (opaque or transparent), JPEG, SVG, PDF (A4/A3/Letter, portrait or landscape), JSON of the whole board, and copy-to-clipboard as an image. With a selection active you can also export just the selected objects — individually or in one file. Import re-opens an exported board JSON (replace) or places an exported object onto the current board (add).

Live collaboration

Go Live (top-right) starts a real-time session: share the link it puts in your address bar and anyone who opens it joins the same room — cursors, presence avatars and edits sync instantly. Concurrent edits merge per element (latest edit wins for the same element), and someone joining late never overwrites the room. Note that while peers are editing, your local undo history resets to keep everyone consistent.
